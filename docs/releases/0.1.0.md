# duinlab-today 0.1.0

duinlab-today is a daily morning paper for The Hague: pictures, sourced reading, a short quiz,
daily games and useful local information in one page.

- Look closely at the day's pictures in a larger viewer, with zoom, swipe and keyboard navigation.
- Tap the moon or a planet to bring its details to the top of the almanac.
- Revisit every quiz question after finishing, with clear corrections for missed answers.
- Keep game and quiz progress in your browser, and open yesterday's paper when you missed a day.

Read it at [today.duinlab.nl](https://today.duinlab.nl/).
The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) lists the release's changes.
