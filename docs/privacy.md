# Privacy statement

duinlab-today keeps your work on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else.
duinlab-today has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

This page covers duinlab-today at [today.duinlab.nl](https://today.duinlab.nl). Simon van Lierde runs
duinlab-today and is responsible for it. "We" on this page means Simon van Lierde.

## What duinlab-today stores on your device

duinlab-today saves your work in your browser's local storage. Nothing is saved until you
start working. It stores:

- The games you opened and the days you opened them. This gives the played marks and streaks.
- Your quiz answers for today, and the days you finished the quiz.
- The day you last saw the sunrise replay, so it plays once a day.

## How to delete it

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

## What our host sees

[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) hosts duinlab-today on Cloudflare Workers. As with
any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details when you load a page. It may log
these to keep the site running and safe, which the law counts as a legitimate interest, and its
privacy policy says for how long. We store no data about visitors of our own.

The Worker has Cloudflare Workers Logs turned on, so Cloudflare keeps request logs for the site in
our account for a few days: the address asked for, the time, and connection details such as your
IP address. We read them only to trace a fault.

## Other sites your browser contacts

duinlab-today loads its scripts, styles and fonts from today.duinlab.nl. The day's pictures load
straight from the museum or news site that published them: NASA, The Metropolitan Museum of Art,
Wikimedia, the Art Institute of Chicago or the Rijksmuseum. On days when NASA's picture is a
video, its preview image comes from the video's host, usually YouTube. Those sites see your IP
address and browser details, as in any image request.

The links to games, sources and the week forecast open other sites only when you select them.
Those sites have their own privacy statements.

## Cookies

duinlab-today sets no cookies, so there's no cookie banner. Local storage holds only what you
asked duinlab-today to keep, and that needs no consent.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we have about you, and to correct it, delete it, limit how we
use it, or stop using it. We never get what you keep in duinlab-today, so we can't see or
change it.

To ask, or with any other question about this page, email
[code@duinlab.nl](mailto:code@duinlab.nl). If you're not happy with our answer, you can
complain to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands, that's the
[Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).
