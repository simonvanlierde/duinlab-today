# Privacy statement

duinlab-today keeps your work on your device. It isn't sent to me or to anyone else.
It has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics.

This page covers [today.duinlab.nl](https://today.duinlab.nl). I'm Simon van Lierde. I run
duinlab-today and I'm responsible for it.

## What duinlab-today stores on your device

It saves a few things in your browser's local storage. The sunrise date is saved on your first
visit. The rest is saved only once you open a game or answer the quiz.

- The games you opened and the days you opened them. This gives the played marks and streaks.
- Your quiz answers for today, and the days you finished the quiz.
- The day you last saw the sunrise replay, so it plays once a day.

## How to delete it

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

## What the host sees

[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) hosts duinlab-today on Cloudflare Workers.
As on any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details when you load a page. It
may log them to keep the site running and safe, which the law counts as a legitimate interest.
Its privacy policy says for how long. I store no data about visitors myself.

Cloudflare Workers Logs is on, so Cloudflare keeps request logs in my account for a few days.
They hold the address asked for, the time, and connection details such as your IP address. I
read them only to trace a fault.

## Other sites your browser contacts

duinlab-today loads its scripts, styles and fonts from today.duinlab.nl.

The day's pictures load straight from the site that serves each one: the museums (The
Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Rijksmuseum), Wikimedia, or the
host NASA links that day, which on a video day is a video host such as YouTube. Those sites see
your IP address and browser details, as in any image request. They also see that the request came
from today.duinlab.nl. Some museums refuse a picture request that doesn't say where it came from.

The server fetches the day's content (weather, tides, pictures, facts) for one fixed place.
Nothing about you goes with those requests.

The links to games, sources and the week forecast open other sites only when you select them.
Those sites have their own privacy statements.

## Cookies

duinlab-today sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Local storage holds only what the
page's own features need, and that needs no consent.

## Your rights

You can ask me to show you the data I have about you, and to correct it, delete it, limit how I
use it, or stop using it. I never receive what you keep in duinlab-today, so I can't see or
change it.

To ask, or with any other question about this page, email
[code@duinlab.nl](mailto:code@duinlab.nl). If you're not happy with my answer, you can complain
to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands, that's the
[Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/).
